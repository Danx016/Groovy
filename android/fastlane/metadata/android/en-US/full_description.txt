
Groovy is a beautiful music streaming application with an Apple Music-inspired UI that adapts to phones and tablets.

Features:
- Ultra-fast music streaming and offline playback
- Apple Music-style UI with automatic dark/light theming
- Powerful search, library browsing, and playlist management
- Full-featured Now Playing screen with lyrics, shuffle, repeat, and queue controls
- Android Auto support and hardware media controls
- Offline playback and caching for listening without a connection
- Rich artist and album metadata with high-res artwork

Groovy is cross-platform (Android, iOS, Windows, Linux, macOS) and optimized for Android with a prebuilt APK available on the project's GitHub releases page.

Try the latest APK from the releases to test new features. Groovy is open source and actively maintained on GitHub.
